Academic Programs

K–5 Traditional & 6–12 Hybrid Programs

Desert Crescent Preparatory Academy offers two distinct academic models to meet diverse learning styles and family preferences.

K–5 Traditional Classroom Model

Small, supportive classrooms with in-person instruction focused on literacy and writing foundations, math fluency and reasoning, science exploration, social studies and global awareness.

6–12 Hybrid Academic Model

Computer-based core classes (Math, ELA, Science, Social Studies) with personalized pacing, on-site paraprofessional academic support, dedicated Sacred Sciences teacher, and college-prep transcript pathway.

4-Day Instructional Week

Monday–Thursday focused learning days. Fridays are used for teacher planning, professional development, student intervention, parent conferences, and optional enrichment.

Islamic Studies & Sacred Sciences

Faith at the center: Qur'an foundations, Arabic exposure, Aqeedah, Fiqh, Seerah, Tafsir foundations, and Islamic ethics and leadership. Islamic values are integrated into daily school life, not treated as an add-on.

Core Academic Areas

  • STEM Focus Curriculum
  • Classical & Conversational Arabic
  • Integrated Quranic Studies
  • Advanced Humanities & Arts
