About Us

Rooted in Values. Rising to Excellence.

Our Mission & Vision

Desert Crescent Preparatory Academy (DCPA) is dedicated to providing a high-quality education that integrates rigorous academics with strong moral values. Our mission is to cultivate curious minds, build resilient character, and foster a lifelong love of learning within a supportive community.

We envision a school where every student is empowered to achieve their full potential, grounded in their heritage and prepared to lead with integrity and compassion in a global society.

Core Values

  • QURAN: Providing spiritual guidance and a moral compass for life.
  • ILM: The pursuit of knowledge as a lifelong journey.
  • AKHLAQ: Developing excellent character and ethical behavior.
  • AMAL: Translating knowledge and values into positive action.

Our Mascot: Noor the Falcon

Noor represents the keen sight, swift action, and noble spirit we encourage in our students. Like the falcon, our students are taught to soar high while remaining rooted in the values that define them.
