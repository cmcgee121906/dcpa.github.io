Welcome from Dr. Christine McGee

Founding Principal

At Desert Crescent Preparatory Academy, we believe that strong schools begin with strong leadership. We are honored to have Dr. Christine McGee serving as our founding Principal.

Dr. McGee brings more than 20 years of experience in education, including 13 years as a classroom teacher and over 7 years in educational leadership as an instructional coach, interventionist, and principal. Throughout her career, she has been committed to creating schools where students are academically challenged, spiritually nurtured, and empowered to reach their full potential.

Dr. McGee holds a Bachelor's Degree in Education, a Master's Degree in Teacher Leadership, and a Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.) in Curriculum and Instruction. Her expertise includes instructional leadership, school improvement, curriculum development, teacher coaching, student achievement, restorative practices, and building positive school cultures.

As Principal, Dr. McGee is passionate about partnering with families to provide an exceptional educational experience rooted in academic excellence and Islamic values. She believes every child deserves a safe, nurturing environment where they are inspired to become lifelong learners, compassionate leaders, and individuals of strong faith and character.

A Leadership Philosophy Built on Faith, Knowledge, Character, and Excellence

Under her leadership, Desert Crescent Preparatory Academy is dedicated to fostering a community where Faith, Knowledge, Character, and Excellence guide every aspect of the student experience. Dr. McGee is excited to welcome families into a school community where every child is known, valued, and encouraged to thrive academically, socially, emotionally, and spiritually.

A Message from Our Principal

“It is both an honor and a privilege to serve as the founding Principal of Desert Crescent Preparatory Academy. I look forward to partnering with our families as we build a school community that prepares students to succeed in this life while remaining firmly grounded in their faith, character, and love of learning.”

— Dr. Christine McGee
