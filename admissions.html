Admissions

Admissions Process

We appreciate your interest in enrolling your child at DCPA. Our school looks to admit curious minds who will actively thrive in our dual-academic environment and positively contribute to our ethical student community. Follow these four simple steps to apply:

1

Attend Open House

Attend an open house or family meeting to learn about DCPA and get your questions answered by our admissions team.

2

Submit Enrollment Packet

Complete and submit the enrollment packet with parent and student information.

3

Provide Required Documents

Submit birth certificate, immunization records, prior school records, and emergency contact information.

4

Receive Enrollment Confirmation

Complete your enrollment and receive confirmation. Welcome to the DCPA family!
