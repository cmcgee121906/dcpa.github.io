Admissions

Admissions Process

We appreciate your interest in enrolling your child at DCPA. Our school looks to admit curious minds who will actively thrive in our dual-academic environment and positively contribute to our ethical student community. Follow these four simple steps to apply:

1

Online Inquiry

Fill out our swift application inquiry form. This allows our admissions desk to record your background, grading preferences, and student interests.

2

Campus Tour

Schedule a private, in-person campus walking tour. You will meet with our principal, see our classrooms in action, and explore our STEM facilities.

3

Application File

Complete our formal application form, uploading previous grading documents, school recommendations, and transcript details.

4

Student Placement Interview

We conduct brief, encouraging placement diagnostic interviews to analyze students' capabilities, character fits, and grade readiness before final acceptances.
